THE STARTER IDEA BOARD

Something to begin with.

These are suggested starting points, ready for you to adapt and propose to the community.

6 starter ideas

STARTER IDEA / WEB

A local business page

A useful mobile website for a small business.

Read the project brief

The idea
Create a clear one-page website that shows what a local business offers and how to contact it.

Skills to bring
Responsive layout, clear content, click-to-contact, accessible navigation.

A useful first outcome
A working page, a mobile screenshot, and a short explanation of the choices.

STARTER IDEA / DATA

A Kerala data story

One question, one dataset, one clear insight.

Read the project brief

The idea
Explore a public dataset about a topic you care about and tell one focused story.

Skills to bring
Data cleaning, chart design, context checking, honest interpretation.

A useful first outcome
One chart, a paragraph explaining it, and notes on source and limitations.

STARTER IDEA / DESIGN

A better everyday screen

Redesign one confusing interaction.

Read the project brief

The idea
Choose a screen people use often and improve one concrete task.

Skills to bring
User research, writing, wireframing, visual design.

A useful first outcome
A before-and-after concept and feedback from one person who tried it.

STARTER IDEA / WEB

A community resource shelf

Help people find useful shared knowledge.

Read the project brief

The idea
Build a simple searchable collection of learning resources with clear labels.

Skills to bring
Frontend development, content organisation, accessibility.

A useful first outcome
A small working collection with search, tags, and original source links.

STARTER IDEA / PEOPLE

A skill-sharing session

Turn what you know into a useful short session.

Read the project brief

The idea
Prepare a 15-minute explanation of one skill, followed by a practical activity.

Skills to bring
Teaching, facilitation, examples, participant feedback.

A useful first outcome
A short outline, one example, and a small exercise everyone can try.

STARTER IDEA / PEOPLE

An idea interview

Understand a real problem before solving it.

Read the project brief

The idea
Talk to someone about a repeated difficulty in their work or studies.

Skills to bring
Listening, note taking, synthesis, problem framing.

A useful first outcome
An anonymised problem summary and three questions to explore next.
