OUR AMBITION

Big enough to connect.
Personal enough to care.

We’re working towards becoming Kerala’s biggest community for curious professionals, makers, and learners. The starting point is simple: give people a place to share ideas and support each other.

Tech is part of our name. Openness is part of our culture. Data, design, business, education, and other fields belong in the conversation too.

How we show up.

  • Introduce yourself and welcome someone new.
  • Keep conversations respectful and relevant.
  • Share opportunities honestly, with clear context.
  • Ask permission before sharing someone’s private information.
  • Coordinate gatherings with admins and participants.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Everyone starts somewhere.

Do I need to work in tech to join?

No. Students, beginners, experienced professionals, and people in other fields are welcome. A willingness to learn and support others matters more than a job title.

Where does the community connect?

Our hub is on WhatsApp, with spaces for Builders, Learning Circle, General, and Announcements. Join the hub to explore the available groups.

Can I suggest a project or a meeting?

Yes. Bring a clear idea to the group, invite feedback, and coordinate meeting plans with the admins. Our Build and Meetups pages help you prepare a useful starting point.

Is this only for people in Kerala?

Kerala is our home and the focus of our ambition. People from elsewhere are welcome to learn, connect, and contribute.

Are there confirmed upcoming events?

Event dates and venues will be shared by the admins in Announcements. The Meetups page currently offers gathering formats and a planning tool.
