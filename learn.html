Web development / STARTER GUIDE

Build your first useful page.

Open the 5-step guide
  1. Pick one person and one problem your page can help with.
  2. Sketch the heading, main action, and three useful pieces of content.
  3. Build the structure with semantic HTML, then add responsive CSS.
  4. Check keyboard navigation, narrow screens, and readable contrast.
  5. Share a working link and ask for feedback on one specific thing.

Data thinking / STARTER GUIDE

Tell a clear story with data.

Open the 5-step guide
  1. Choose a small public or self-created dataset you can explain.
  2. Write one question before making any chart.
  3. Check missing values, inconsistent units, and duplicate records.
  4. Make one simple chart that helps answer your question.
  5. Explain your conclusion and what the data cannot tell you.

Design / STARTER GUIDE

Make something easier to use.

Open the 5-step guide
  1. Choose a familiar screen and name one confusing moment.
  2. Sketch a simpler version with a clear primary action.
  3. Check the size, contrast, and wording of important controls.
  4. Ask someone to try the task without coaching them.
  5. Share the before, the change, and what you learned.

Communication / STARTER GUIDE

Ask better questions.

Open the 5-step guide
  1. Give a short description of your goal.
  2. Explain what you have tried and what happened.
  3. Include a small example, screenshot, or relevant error.
  4. Ask one focused question that someone can answer.
  5. Share the solution afterwards so the next person benefits.

Collaboration / STARTER GUIDE

Start a tiny team project.

Open the 5-step guide
  1. Pick a problem you can explore in a week.
  2. Agree on a small first deliverable and who it helps.
  3. Separate the work into clear responsibilities.
  4. Choose one place to track progress and decisions.
  5. Show a demo and write down the next useful improvement.

Career growth / STARTER GUIDE

Give useful project feedback.

Open the 5-step guide
  1. Ask what kind of feedback the creator wants.
  2. Try the main action before judging the appearance.
  3. Describe what you observed, without making assumptions.
  4. Suggest one concrete improvement and explain why.
  5. Mention one thing that worked well and should be kept.
