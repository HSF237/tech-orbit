# Tech Orbit artwork

Seven original editorial illustrations generated with the built-in image tool. Originals are retained; optimized WebP files are used by the website.

## community

Website assets: `community.webp` and `community-small.webp`.

Use case: illustration-story. Create a finished editorial illustration for the Tech Orbit community website, landscape 4:3 composition. A diverse group of Indian young adult professionals around a giant abstract orbit in a leafy Kerala creative courtyard. One holds a laptop, another a sketchbook, another chart cards. Playful varied gestures, natural human warmth, interconnected flowing lines. Style: contemporary hand-drawn flat vector-like editorial illustration with confident thin dark outlines, playful exaggerated proportions, gently textured paper, charming expressive faces, bold organic shapes, premium magazine art. Palette consistent across the set: deep indigo ink, periwinkle blue, warm saffron, coral, sage and warm cream #f6f3ec background. Designed as one standalone website illustration, generous margins, all figures fully contained. No photorealism, no 3D, no watermark, no typography, no logos, no website mockup.
